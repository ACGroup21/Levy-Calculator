What lands at your end

The page writes the subject line, not the sender. Every brief arrives in the same shape, so one inbox rule catches all of them — which is the difference between a channel you can rely on and a pile you have to sort.

  • You know whose it isName, work email, employer and programme travel with the file. Nothing needs chasing before you can start.
  • It routes itselfEmployer and programme sit in the subject line, so briefs file into the right client folder on arrival.
  • The reply has somewhere to goThe work email is the return address, so the step guide goes straight back to the apprentice who asked for it.

Example of the arrival format

Three steps, and the apprentice does two of them

01
Post the brief

The slot is the file input. Click it, drop a file on the box, or tab to it — PDF, Word or text.

02
Fill the label

Name, work email, employer, and the programme picked from the live list of apprenticeship standards.

03
It comes to you

Labelled, attributed, and ready for the Assignment Builder to turn into a step guide and KSB map.

Where this is up to

The box itself is finished and live. It is not yet connected to an inbox — until a form service is chosen it validates and confirms without sending, so it can be shown to anyone without routing real learner data anywhere. Post a file in the frame above and you will see the whole flow; nothing leaves the page.

Fundable · light mode — background

Six light backgrounds, tested the honest way.

My earlier attempts failed because I kept proposing pictures — sky, weather, neon geometry — for a surface someone stares at for an hour while reading money. Dark mode's constellation works because it is sparse, structural and nearly subliminal; you look past it, not at it. These are judged on that standard.

Every panel shows a dense, realistic slab of the app — cards, figures, a table, and text sitting directly on the background — at the opacity it would actually ship at. If a background is too busy, it will show here.

6 · Nothing at all the control

Just the pale sky. No texture, no motion.

Included deliberately: compare every option against this. If a background is not clearly better than plain, it is costing you attention for nothing.
How I would judge these: look at the £61,776 in each panel, not at the background. The one where you notice the number fastest is the right answer.

My recommendation is 1 (ledger ruling), with 4 (constellation) as the strong alternative if you would rather both themes share one idea. I have included 6 (nothing) as a genuine option — the constellation earns its place in dark mode because dark surfaces look dead without texture, and that argument is much weaker on white.

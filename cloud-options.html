Fundable · light mode — sky & clouds

Six skies to sit the app on.

Dark mode has stars on near-black; light mode should be the same idea inverted — white clouds on a blue sky, not grey clouds on white. Each panel shows the real app furniture floating on the sky so you can judge legibility, not just prettiness.

Clouds drift slowly and sit behind everything; all six are click-through and freeze under reduced-motion.

6 · Barely there

The faintest blue with clouds you notice only if you look for them.

Safest for hours of use and it will never date — but it is close to what you already rejected, so it may not read as blue enough.
My recommendation: 1 (Pale sky), with 5 as the alternative if you want the blue to be unmistakable. One is blue enough to read as sky while leaving the white cards and the money figures completely dominant; five is the bolder statement and the truer mirror of the dark theme, at some cost over a long working session.

One practical note: the app scrolls for about eleven screens, so a top-to-bottom gradient (option 2 or 3) either runs out or has to be fixed to the viewport. A flat sky with drifting clouds (1, 4, 5, 6) behaves the same at any scroll depth, which is why I favour it for a tool rather than a landing page.

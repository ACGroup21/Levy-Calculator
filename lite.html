Funding band — the maximum government will fund for that standard, drawn 80% over the practical period and 20% at end-point assessment.
Co-investment — 5% of the band for programmes starting before August 2026, 25% from August 2026 onward. Learners aged 16–21 with a non-levy employer are 100% funded.
Levy — your pot plus twelve months of incoming levy. You co-invest only on training the pot cannot cover.
Off-the-job time — 6 hours a week at £18/hour across the programme. It is real payroll cost and never funded, which is why it is shown as your money.

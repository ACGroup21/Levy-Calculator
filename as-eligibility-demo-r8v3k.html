The moving parts

Every animation and reading aid in the Ann Summers Eligibility Checker, shown one at a time. Replay them, slow them down, and switch the whole page between light and dark.

1The splash screen

What a manager sees on their first visit. A pink LED spark runs round the whole word at once, the word lights up, then the spark passes to the apple. “Powered by Fundable” appears where the button will be, then turns into the “Click to enter” button. Return visits skip it.

2The LED spark on its own

3The apple heartbeat

The apple pops in with a glow, then keeps a gentle double-beat while the splash is open.

4The moving background

Soft brand-colour light drifting behind the whole app, very slowly (20–30 second cycles), so it never competes with the form. This page's own background is it. It stays still for anyone whose device asks for reduced motion.

5Light and dark

The app follows the device's theme automatically (“Auto”), and anyone can pick Light or Dark. Every colour has a version for each.

6Screen cover and reading ruler

A coloured overlay that can make text easier to read with dyslexia or visual stress, plus a ruler that shades everything except the line you're on. Pick a colour, then move your pointer over the text.

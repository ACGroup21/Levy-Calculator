The moving parts

Every animation and reading aid in the Ann Summers Eligibility Checker, shown one at a time. Replay them, slow them down, and switch the whole page between light and dark.

1The splash screen

What a manager sees on their first visit. The outline of each letter draws itself in turn and fills with colour, then the apple pops in. “Powered by Fundable” appears where the button will be, then dissolves into the “Click to enter” button from left to right, over a slowly shifting aurora background. Return visits skip it.

3The apple heartbeat

After the letters, the apple draws its outline, pops in with a glow, then keeps a gentle double-beat while the splash is open.

4The moving background

Behind the app: soft brand-colour light drifting very slowly (20–30 second cycles), so it never competes with the form. This page's own background is it. The splash screen uses the aurora instead (see card 1). Both stay still for anyone whose device asks for reduced motion.

Background

5Light and dark

The app follows the device's theme automatically (“Auto”), and anyone can pick Light or Dark. Every colour has a version for each.

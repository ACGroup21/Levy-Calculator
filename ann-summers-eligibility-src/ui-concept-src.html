What changes in this concept

  • Headings in Montserrat ExtraBold with the magenta highlighter, as in the Levy Report.
  • Sections open with black slab banners numbered 01, 02, 03; the journey bar uses the same numbers.
  • Inputs become underlined fields and square Yes/No toggles, sharper and more editorial than rounded boxes.
  • Results lead with a black verdict panel, then numbered key points, a black timeline panel and half-donut gauges for who pays.
  • Unchanged: every rule, figure, the JD matcher, dark mode and the reading aids; this is styling only.

Fundable · light mode — atmosphere

Four atmospheric backgrounds, actually rendered.

My last attempt used blurred circles, which read as smudges rather than sky. These are drawn on canvas with layered noise, so they have real cloud texture and depth — and they move continuously rather than looping a short animation.

All four sit behind the app, are click-through, and stop under reduced-motion. The app furniture on top is real, so judge legibility as well as look.

4 · Layered depth

Three cloud layers moving at different speeds, so nearer cloud outruns the far bank.

Gives genuine parallax depth. The most alive of the four — and the most motion, which is the trade-off.
My recommendation: 3 (atmospheric haze) for the app, 1 (volumetric) for the demo and landing pages. The app is where people read numbers for an hour, so atmosphere without imagery is the right restraint; the marketing pages can afford real sky because nobody is working in them.

Honest note on cost: all four render continuously, unlike the CSS version which the browser handles for free. They are drawn at low resolution and scaled up, which keeps them cheap, and they pause when the tab is hidden — but on an old laptop option 4 is the one most likely to be felt.

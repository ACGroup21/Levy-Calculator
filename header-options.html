Header spacing — three ways to organise it

The complaint: the mode switch is jammed against the wordmark, so a utility control reads as part of the brand lockup. All three options below fix that; they differ in where the controls live and what the spacing says.

The organising rule underneath all three: gaps encode grouping — 8px between things that belong together, 24–32px between things that don't. Right now every gap in the header is 8px, so nothing is grouped and the eye can't tell brand from control.

Option 1Hairline rule

Keeps the switch on the left where you put it, and draws an explicit line where the brand ends and the controls begin. Most literal fix — but it adds a piece of furniture to a bar that currently has none.

Option 2 — recommendedGrouped by job

Switch stays left but gets 32px of air, so it reads as its own thing rather than part of the lockup. Save moves right to sit beside Reset, because they're both plan actions — one saves your work, one destroys it, and they belong in the same cluster. The right flank then reads in three clear beats: plan actions · menu · provenance. No new furniture, and every gap earns its width.

My recommendation: Option 2

It fixes the actual complaint — the switch gets real separation from the wordmark — without adding a divider that no other part of the app uses, and without undoing your decision to keep the switch on the left.

The reason I'd move Save across is that Save and Reset are the same kind of thing: both act on the plan you're holding. Right now one sits with the brand and one sits with the menu, which is why the bar reads as scattered even at matched gaps. Put them together and the header states four things in order — who this is · what mode you're in · where you are · what you can do to the plan.

One caveat worth naming: Save and Reset next to each other puts a green button beside a red destructive one. Reset already asks for confirmation, and the colour difference is stark, so I think 8px is fine — but if you'd rather not have them touching, say so and I'll push Reset out to its own cluster.

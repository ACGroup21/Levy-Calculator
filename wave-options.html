4 · The same idea, in light mode

The mesh drawn in blues on the pale sky instead of glowing on black.

Proves the effect survives inversion — it reads as a blueprint rather than a neon visualiser, which arguably suits a funding tool better.
My recommendation: use this on the demo and landing pages, and the quiet version (4) at low opacity in the app. The effect is genuinely striking, and it earns its place on a page whose job is to impress in ten seconds. In the app — where someone reads figures for an hour — the same energy competes with the numbers.

The honest tension: this is the most "AI-generated tech" look of anything we have built, and your actual differentiator is being verifiable rather than futuristic. Rendering it in your own two brand colours instead of the rainbow keeps it yours; going full spectrum would make it look like everyone else's landing page.

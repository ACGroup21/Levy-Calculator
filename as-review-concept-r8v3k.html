Review the report: what should happen when you click it

Today the button often seems to do nothing, because the result is usually already at the top. These three options all make it obvious: the Eligible card flashes, and a pink arrow on the right flashes with a trail until you scroll. They differ only in the pop-up badge.

1Pick an option: A, B or C.
2Click Review the report (left, under 05).
3Watch the results column on the right: the card flash, the pink arrow, the badge.
4Scroll the results column with your mouse wheel. Or press ▶ Play it for me.

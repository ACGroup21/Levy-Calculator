The browser's own picker

What the app uses now, restyled to match. The field is ours; the pop-up calendar is drawn by the browser (grey on Windows, a scroll wheel on phones), so it can only follow light or dark mode.

Pick a start date to see the check.

Option B · GOV.UK pattern

Day / Month / Year boxes

Three small boxes, no calendar. GOV.UK uses this for dates people already know, like a date of birth.

Option C · custom

Smart calendar

Our own calendar that shows the rules before you pick: weekends and bank holidays greyed, the Christmas shutdown and your busy months shaded, the earliest start date and the deadline ringed, and dates too late for funding struck through.
